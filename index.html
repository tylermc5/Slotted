How it works

Three steps. Real money in your YouTube workflow.

No new tools to learn. No data to manually export. Slotted plugs into the surfaces your team already uses every day.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Don't see yours? Ask us anything →

What is a Dynamic Brand Segment, exactly?

DBS is a new YouTube format (launching April 2026) that lets creators insert a 60-second host-read sponsorship segment into their long-form videos that can be swapped, resold, or geo-targeted independently of the main video. Google built the delivery rail; Slotted is where the deals get done.

Does Slotted replace my agent or manager?

No. Slotted is the marketplace and software layer — the discovery, pricing, contracts, escrow, and reporting. Your manager handles strategy, relationships, and the deals Slotted can't see. Most creators keep both.

How do payments work?

Brands fund escrow when a deal is signed. We release funds to creators when the slot goes live in YouTube Studio (or, optionally, when a view threshold is hit). Payouts run through Stripe Connect. Standard creator payment terms.

Is this just programmatic ads in disguise?

No. Per Google's own FAQ: "This is not open exchange programmatic inventory. It's still a manual, direct deal between the Brand and Creator." Slotted enforces direct deals and creator approval on every booking.

Can I block certain brands or verticals?

Yes. Creators set blocklists by brand, vertical, or competitor of an existing deal. Brands set safety screens by creator type, content category, or audience profile. Both sides keep full approval rights on every match.

What's the minimum spend for brands?

Self-serve starts at $10K per campaign. Agency-tier customers and enterprise sales-supported accounts have no minimum. Most first campaigns land between $50K and $250K.
