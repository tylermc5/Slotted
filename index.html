How a slot becomes a spot

List. Produce. Resell.

No new tools to learn. No data to manually export. Slotted plugs into the surfaces your team already uses every day.

① List

Connect, list, set capacity

Creators connect YouTube Studio. Available DBS slots surface as a calendar across new uploads and back catalog. Publish your endorsement profile — verticals, turnaround, capacity, banned categories. Set base CPMs or accept the platform yield curve.

② Brief & Produce

Brand briefs. Creator produces.

Buyers submit a structured brief — brand, product, key messages, CTA, audience — not a finished creative. The creator records the read in their own voice, often delivering 2–3 versions. Both sides approve before go-live. Escrow funds on signature.

③ Run, re-license, resell

Live, measured, and back on market

Reads go live in YouTube Studio. Performance syncs from Google Ads in real time. Swap between produced versions in two clicks. When a term ends, the slot reopens — and existing reads re-license when new briefs match.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Don't see yours? Ask us anything →

Who actually creates the ad?

The creator does — always. The buyer submits a brief (brand, product, key messages, CTA, audience screens). The creator records the read in their own voice, against the brief, often delivering 2–3 versions. Both sides approve before go-live. The creative is never brand-supplied. The trust transfer from creator to brand is the entire reason DBS clears at 3–5× display CPMs.

Is this just programmatic ads in disguise?

No. Slotted brings the buying motion of programmatic — public clearing prices, yield curves, geo tenancy, swap, native measurement — to a market where the creative is creator-produced and every booking is a direct deal with creator approval. Per Google's own documentation, DBS inventory is not open-exchange programmatic. The motion is exchange-cleared. The creative isn't.

How long does production take?

Depends on the creator. Each creator publishes their standard turnaround in their endorsement profile — typical range is 5–10 business days from brief approval to delivered read. Buyers see turnaround upfront and can filter for faster cycles. Mid-flight swap between produced versions is instant. CTA-only swaps (URL, promo code) are instant. Commissioning a new version follows the creator's turnaround.

What is re-licensing?

When an existing creator-read fits a new buyer's brief — same product category, compatible messaging, creator-approved — it can be re-licensed at a discount instead of re-produced from scratch. The exchange runs a brief-match score against every existing read in the creator's library. Re-licensing is always opt-in for the creator. It's how production compounds into IP rather than expiring with each campaign.

What is a Dynamic Brand Segment, exactly?

DBS is a new YouTube format (launching April 2026, US geo-targeting May 2026) that turns the 60-second creator-read sponsorship inside a long-form video into a standardized, swappable, geo-targeted, resellable slot. Google built the delivery and measurement rails. They explicitly opted out of the transaction layer. Slotted is the transaction layer.

Why isn't a CreatorIQ or Captiv8 the right place for this?

Their data model is the campaign, not the slot or the read. Influencer platforms manage creator relationships and run bespoke per-deal workflows. None of them clear inventory, run public yield curves, route geo multi-tenancy, run brief-match re-licensing, or sync measurement to Google Ads natively. Retrofitting any of those into a campaign-shaped data model is a 12–24 month rebuild. We started slot-shaped, read-aware.

Does Slotted replace my agent or manager?

No. Slotted is the marketplace and software layer — discovery, brief intake, contracts, escrow, reporting, re-licensing. Strategy, relationships, and the bespoke deals that don't fit the slot model still belong to humans. Most creators keep both.

How do payments work?

Brands fund escrow when a brief is approved. We release funds to creators when the read goes live in YouTube Studio — or optionally when a view threshold is hit. Payouts run through Stripe Connect with standard creator payment terms.

Can I block certain brands or verticals?

Yes. Creators publish their endorsement profile upfront — verticals they'll read for, verticals they refuse, competitor blocks, banned categories. Buyers set safety screens by creator type, content category, and audience profile. Both sides keep full approval rights on every brief and every read.

What's the minimum spend?

Self-serve starts at $10K per campaign. Trading-desk seat and enterprise sales accounts have no minimum. Most first campaigns clear between $50K and $250K across multiple reads.
